\noindent\footnotesize
\textbf{Notes.}
(1) \CBa{} ``regular'' requires a regular initial task network; Bercher et al.\ allow an arbitrary one.
The two readings differ only for strong OD (EXPSPACE-complete vs.\ undecidable).
(2) The literal strong cyclic definition of \CBb{} counts forever-looping policies as solutions;
the results use the \emph{fair} reading \DBC{}, which coincides with strong on acyclic problems.
(3) Column-wide gaps, completed \DBC{}: the strong LD compilation \CBa{Lem 4.6} \I{} (extended to negative
preconditions), \CBb{Prop 2} \I{}, and the unstated lemma that delaying decompositions never hurts.
(4) Also formalised: REG $\cap$ ACYC $\cap$ TO is NP-complete. \DBc{Thm 1} (chosen-length games) is
\Pp{}; it is not needed.
